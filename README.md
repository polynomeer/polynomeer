### Make Non-Polynomial Polynomial — Solving Hard Problems the Easy Way.

I’m a backend developer who simplifies complexity and finds clarity in chaos.

I specialize in identifying hidden bottlenecks in systems—whether it’s traffic overload, data inconsistency, or sluggish batch performance—and transforming them into elegant, efficient solutions.
My strength lies in breaking down intricate logic into clean architecture and maintainable code.

With experience in Java and Spring Boot, I’ve worked on scalable APIs, high-throughput data flows, and robust system design. I thrive on solving deep-rooted problems alongside my team and building systems that just work—even under pressure.

Technology can be complex, but users shouldn’t feel that complexity.
That’s why I’m committed to making hard problems feel simple.
